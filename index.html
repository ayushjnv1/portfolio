Key Achievements at DrinkPrime

Auto-Routing: Automated Technician Job Assignment

Designed and built an end-to-end auto-routing system for field technician scheduling. Every morning it triggers an automated IVR call and WhatsApp message to confirm the customer is present at the service location; responses are processed through a queue and used to mark customers available in real time. Available jobs are then auto-assigned to the nearest technician based on live technician and job locations using best-route optimization, and at the end of the day each technician's last job is chosen by optimizing for proximity between their current location, the nearest pending job, and their route home. A priority-assignment layer lets high-priority jobs jump the queue, with added incentives for technicians who complete them.

Technologies: Java, Spring Boot, IVR/Telephony API, WhatsApp Business API, RabbitMQ, Geolocation & Route Optimization, PostgreSQL

Impact: Fully automated customer availability confirmation and technician job assignment, enabling a reduction of the CX and Ops team size by approx. 50% while improving route efficiency and technician utilization
